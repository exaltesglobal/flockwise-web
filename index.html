Built for Indian churches from day one

Not a foreign tool with India bolted on. The things your treasurer and auditor ask about are already handled.

UPI giving and AutoPay

Members give by UPI, card or net banking through Razorpay, straight to the church’s own account. Monthly giving runs on UPI AutoPay.

80G receipts and Form 10BD

Receipt numbers by financial year, yearly statements members can print, and the donor export your CA needs for Form 10BD.

FCRA-safe

Gifts from foreign citizens and OCI holders are routed to your FCRA account, or politely refused if you aren’t registered.

Your language

The member app speaks English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Kannada.

DPDP consent

Each person chooses whether to get email, SMS or WhatsApp, and whether to appear in the directory. Flockwise respects it every time.

Cash and cheque too

Record Sunday offerings by cash, cheque or bank transfer next to online gifts, with the ₹2,000 cash rule for 80G applied for you.
